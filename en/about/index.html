About Tridan

Tridan is a team of software developers, engineers and problem solvers based in Belgrade, Serbia. We build custom software for companies, with a focus on full-stack development, integrations, cloud and AI. Every project is a collaboration with the client at the center, from the first idea to long-term support.

01

Who is behind Tridan?

Tridan was founded by Nemanja Milivojevic, who leads the team and the company's technical direction. He writes and records videos about software development and AI on his YouTube channel.

The same company also builds Tridan AI, a platform for AI agents for calls and chat. Training is delivered by Tridan Academy, a separate company.

02

How does Tridan work?

  • Collaborative team: developers, designers and strategists work directly with you from start to finish.
  • Experience across industries: biometrics, payments, marketing, cloud and blockchain.
  • Agile and flexible: the process adapts to your needs while the project runs.
  • Quality: every solution is tested for reliability and security.
  • Long-term partnership: support and upgrades so the software stays scalable.

AI integration for companies

AI integration is bringing artificial intelligence models, such as large language models, into existing software and business processes. Tridan connects AI to your applications, data and tools through APIs, so the AI does a concrete job: answers questions, processes documents or automates steps in a process.

01

What can an AI integration do?

  • A chat assistant on your website or in an internal app that answers from your data.
  • Document processing and data extraction from emails, contracts or forms.
  • Natural language search across internal knowledge, manuals and documentation.
  • Automation of repetitive steps in a process, with human review where needed.
  • Connecting AI services to existing applications through APIs.
02

When is a ready-made product better than custom development?

For AI agents that answer phone calls and website chat there is a ready-made platform: Tridan AI. Custom development makes sense when AI has to work inside your own applications, with your databases and specific business rules.

03

How does Tridan approach data and security?

Before development we agree which data the model may see, who has access, which AI service is used and where data is processed. Access is limited to what the task needs, and answers and actions are logged for review. Where a mistake has consequences, the AI proposes and a person approves.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a program based on a language model that does more than answer questions: it carries out steps in a process, such as reading data from a system, filling in forms, booking appointments or forwarding a request to the right person. The agent works within the tools and rules Tridan gives it. Ready-made AI agents for calls and chat are offered by the Tridan AI platform.

Does our data go to a public AI model?

That depends on the chosen AI service and deployment, and it is agreed before the project starts. Tridan selects the model provider, the processing region and the data usage terms together with you, and the model receives only the data needed for the task. When data must not leave your infrastructure, we consider models that run on your own servers or in your own cloud account.

Where can I learn how to use AI safely in a company?

Training on artificial intelligence is delivered by Tridan Academy, a separate company dedicated to education. Its programmes cover applying AI in business, managing AI systems and regulation such as the EU AI Act, including preparation for ISO standards. Tridan builds and integrates the systems, while Tridan Academy trains the people who use and oversee them.

Tridan provides four custom software development services: web applications, integrations and APIs, DevOps and cloud, and AI integration. Each service can be a project of its own or part of a larger solution, and the same team follows the project from architecture to production and maintenance.

How do you choose the right service for a project?

If you are building a new product or internal tool, start with web application development. If systems already exist but do not share data, an integration solves it. If releases are slow or infrastructure is unstable, the answer is DevOps. If you want AI to do a concrete job inside your processes, you need an AI integration. When you are unsure, describe the problem and we propose an approach.

What do you get in every engagement?

  • Tailored solution: software designed for your needs, from architecture to deployment.
  • Agile approach: short cycles, early visible results and a flexible plan.
  • Quality assurance: reliability and security testing before every release.
  • Long-term partnership: maintenance, updates and new features after launch.
  • Scalability: an architecture that keeps up with growing users, data and teams.
